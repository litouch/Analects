八佾篇 第三

3.13王孙贾⑴问曰：“与其媚于奥，宁媚于灶⑵，何谓也？”子曰：“不然；获罪于天，无所祷也⑶。”

白话本 (杨伯峻)

王孙贾问道：“『与其巴结房屋里西南角的神，宁可巴结灶君司命，』这两句话是什么意思？”孔子道：“不对；若是得罪了上天，祈祷也没用。”

English (Legge)

Wang Sun Jia asked, saying, "What is the meaning of the saying, 'It is better to pay court to the furnace than to the southwest corner?'" The Master said, "Not so. He who offends against Heaven has none to whom he can pray."

宪问篇 第十四

14.19子言卫灵公之无道也，康子曰：“夫如是，奚而⑴不丧？”孔子曰：“仲叔圉⑵治宾客，祝鮀治宗庙，王孙贾治军旅。夫如是，奚其丧？”

白话本 (杨伯峻)

孔子讲到卫灵公的昏乱，康子道：“既然这样，为什么不败亡？”孔子道：“他有仲叔圉接待宾客，祝鮀管理祭祀，王孙贾统率军队，像这样，怎么会败亡？”

English (Legge)

The Master was speaking about the unprincipled course of the duke Ling of Wei, when Qi Kang said, "Since he is of such a character, how is it he does not lose his state?" Confucius said, "The Zhong Shu Yu has the superintendence of his guests and of strangers; the litanist, Tuo, has the management of his ancestral temple; and Wang Sun Jia has the direction of the army and forces - with such officers as these, how should he lose his state?"
